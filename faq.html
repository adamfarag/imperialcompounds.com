Ordering & product

Retatrutide ships as lyophilized (freeze-dried) powder in a sealed glass vial — 15 mg per vial, sold by the single vial at $100 CAD. Volume pricing is available for larger orders — contact us.
All Imperial Compounds products are intended strictly for in-vitro laboratory research and development. They are not for human or animal consumption, and are not drugs, foods, cosmetics, or medical devices. By ordering, you affirm you are a qualified researcher handling them lawfully.
We ship discreetly Canada-wide from Edmonton, Alberta, and local delivery is available within Edmonton. We do not ship internationally at this time.
Lyophilized vials are stable refrigerated and should be kept cool, dry and out of light. Reconstitution and handling protocols are the responsibility of the receiving laboratory.

Testing & COA

Every batch is independently tested by an accredited third-party laboratory for identity and purity before it ships. We don't grade our own homework — the report comes from the lab, not from us. How our testing works →
Email the lot number printed on your vial label to orders@imperialcompounds.com and we reply with the certificate of analysis issued for that exact batch.
A missing or mismatched lot is a red flag worth reporting. Contact us before proceeding — testing exists precisely so nothing unverified reaches a bench.

Place your order at checkout; once we confirm it, you receive the payment address for your chosen coin (BTC, ETH or USDC). We ship when the transaction confirms on-chain.
No. Checkout is guest-only — placing an order sends us an email with your order details, and we reply to confirm payment and shipping. We collect just the name, email and city needed to fulfil it.
