Why third-party?

Vendor numbers aren't proof. Independent labs are.

Anyone can print a purity figure on their own letterhead, so each batch goes to an independent, accredited laboratory — the report comes from the lab, not from us. If a batch doesn't meet standard, it doesn't ship.
